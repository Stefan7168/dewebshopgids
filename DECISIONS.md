A shop stays `disputed` after a no-change reply until that job exists. Runs 4 times a day at :15 past 01, 07, 13 and 19 Amsterdam time; with no open disputes it writes one run row and stops.

- 2026-10-28: The nightly pipeline (task 15b) is one workflow, `pipeline.yml`, that runs `scripts/pipeline/nightly.ts` in this order: promote candidates, collect score batches, crawl, domain age, submit a new batch. Each step writes its own `agent_runs` row, and a failing step does not stop the next one. The run ends with a `health_checks` row (`failed_runs`, ok or fail). It reads with the service client and returns only timestamps and statuses. `/api/revalidate` accepts only a Bearer `REVALIDATE_SECRET` and site paths (`/`, `/webshop/*`, `/categorie/*`, `/gids/*`, `/over`).
