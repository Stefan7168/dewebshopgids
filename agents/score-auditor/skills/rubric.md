# Auditor rubric (prompt version a1)

Score from the page text only. Use whole numbers. Give points only for what the pages show.

| Sub-score | Max | How to score |
|---|---|---|
| maker | 30 | 30: the shop makes its products itself (own workshop, made to order by them). 20: sells mainly its own brand made by others. 5 to 10: some own products in a mostly third-party range. 0: resells other brands. |
| focus | 20 | 20: one product type. 12: a few related types. 4: a whole home category. 0: many unrelated categories. |
| identity | 20 | 7 if a person behind the shop is named, 7 if a street address is shown, 6 if a KvK number is shown. Do not count time online: the tool adds it from the measured domain age (4 at 10+ years, 2 at 5+ years, max 20 in total). |
| scale | 20 | From the product count fact: 20 under 300 products, falling evenly to 0 at 5,000. Unknown count: 0. |
| origin | 10 | 4 handmade, 3 own atelier or workshop, 3 made in NL, 2 made in EU (only without made in NL). Max 10. |

`--marketplace true` when the pages show third-party sellers ("verkocht door"), a mass-market platform, or dropship signs (generic texts, long delivery from outside the EU). The tool then caps the total at 30, as the pipeline does.

Be stricter than generous: a missing proof is 0 points. Your job is to find scores that are too high.
