# Judge a shop for Eten & drinken (`eten-drinken`)

Sub-categories: `koffie-thee`, `chocolade-koek`, `kaas-delicatessen`, `honing`. Other food makers (sauces, jams, bread, spices) get the top level `eten-drinken`.

Signals that point here: branderij, koffie, thee, chocolade, chocolatier, bonbon, koek, bakkerij, kaas, delicatessen, honing, imker, saus, jam, specerij.

Always skip:
- Shops whose main product is alcohol (wine, beer, spirits). Alcohol needs age checks (Alcoholwet) and has its own advertising code; it stays out of the directory (DECISIONS.md, 2026-10-29).
- Supplements, slimming products and anything sold with health claims.
- Supermarkets, meal boxes, wholesalers, and resellers of big brands.

A coffee roaster that also sells machines is fine when the beans are its own.
