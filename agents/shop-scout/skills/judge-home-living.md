# Judge whether a shop is Home & Living

Signals in the domain or name that point to Home & Living: meubel, wonen, interieur, lamp, licht, verlichting, kussen, plaid, vloerkleed, karpet, servies, keuken, bed, slaap, tuin, kaars, vaas, design, atelier, hout.

Signals against: mode, kleding, schoenen, sieraden, elektronica, telefoon, auto, parts, supplements, groothandel, b2b, "via bol.com", marketplace names.

When in doubt, skip. A missed shop comes back next week; a wrong one costs Stefan a rejection.
