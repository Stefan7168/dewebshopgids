# Judge a shop for Sieraden & accessoires (`sieraden-accessoires`)

Sub-categories: `sieraden`, `tassen-leer`, `sjaals-mutsen`.

This category is for makers: goldsmiths, silversmiths, jewellery ateliers, leather workers, knitters. Signals that point here: goudsmid, edelsmid, zilversmid, atelier, sieraden, ringen, trouwringen, leer, leder, tassen, portemonnee, sjaal, muts, gebreid.

Always skip:
- Fashion stores and chains, watch dealers, and shops that resell many brands.
- Etsy-only sellers or shops without their own domain.
- Skincare, soap and cosmetics (claims rules under the Cosmetics Regulation; rejected as a category).

The name alone rarely proves "own atelier". Propose a shop only when the name or domain points to a maker (atelier, goudsmid, edelsmid, a maker's workshop name); the crawl and the score check the rest.
