# Judge a shop for Tuin & buiten (`tuin-buiten`)

Sub-categories: `tuinmeubels`, `potten-plantenbakken`, `buitenverlichting`, `vuur-bbq`, `tuinaccessoires`. Growers (bulbs, roses, seeds, plants) get the top level `tuin-buiten`: no sub-category fits them.

Signals that point here: tuin, buiten, terras, balkon, lounge, parasol, plantenbak, pot, kweker, bollen, rozen, zaden, vuurschaal, bbq, buitenkeuken, buitenlamp.

Signals against: tools and machines (grasmaaier, gereedschap, hogedrukreiniger), pools and spas, sheds and building materials, garden centres that resell big brands, dealers-only brands without their own webshop, "groothandel".

A shop that is both home and garden (outdoor furniture brand that also sells indoor pieces): pick the category of its main range. If you cannot tell, skip.
